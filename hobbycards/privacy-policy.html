HobbyCards Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

HobbyCards lets you make simple contact cards and swap them with people nearby. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent to us.

Information you enter

You can enter a name, a title, and contact details (for example a phone number, email address or social media handle) on your cards. Cards you receive from other people contain the same kinds of information. All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.

What we collect

Nothing. HobbyCards has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of its own. The app does not request internet access, and we never receive your cards or any other information about you.

Sharing cards

A card leaves your phone only when you choose to share it:

  • NFC tap: the card is sent directly to the other phone when you hold the phones together.
  • QR code: the card is shown as a QR code for the other person to scan.
  • Share text: the card is passed to the app you pick from Android's share menu (such as a messaging app). That app's own privacy policy applies to what happens next.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan QR codes for cards, and only while the scanner is open. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or sent anywhere. Scanning uses Google's ML Kit barcode scanner, provided through Google Play services. Google may collect limited diagnostic information about how that component performs; see ML Kit's data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Backups

If you have Android's backup turned on, your cards may be included in your device's backup to your Google account, like other app data. This is controlled by your Android settings, not by HobbyCards.

Deleting your information

You can delete any card in the app. Uninstalling HobbyCards, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all cards from your phone.

Children

HobbyCards is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
