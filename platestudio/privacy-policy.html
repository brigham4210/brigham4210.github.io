Plate Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Plate Studio is an app for mocking up US vanity license plates. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone.

Data collected by the developerNone
Data shared with third partiesNone
Accounts or sign-inNone
Ads, analytics or crash reportingNone
Where your data livesOn your device only

What the app stores

Plate Studio saves the following in the app's local storage on your device:

  • Plates you save, including their text, state, colors and the sections you group them into
  • Logos and background images you add, downscaled to keep storage small
  • Settings such as theme and layout preferences

None of this is sent to the developer or anyone else. Fonts and artwork are built into the app, so it does not need to load anything from the internet.

Images you add

When you add a logo or background, Android lets you pick an image. Plate Studio reads only the image you choose and keeps it on your device.

Exporting plates

When you export a plate as a PNG or JPG, the app writes the image to its own cache and opens Android's share sheet. The image goes only to the app or destination you pick; Plate Studio does not upload it anywhere itself.

Permissions

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET) is part of the app framework. Plate Studio does not send your data over the network.

Plate Studio does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or storage permissions.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your plates stay on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. If Android's backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings, not by the developer.

Children

Plate Studio is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
