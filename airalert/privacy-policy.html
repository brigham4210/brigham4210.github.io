AirAlert Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

AirAlert is a workout tracker for a 15-week vertical jump training program. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone.

Data collected by the developerNone
Data shared with third partiesNone
Accounts or sign-inNone
Ads, analytics or crash reportingNone
Where your data livesOn your device only

What the app stores

AirAlert saves the following on your device so it can show your progress:

  • Your program start date and the exercises you check off
  • Vertical jump heights you choose to log
  • Settings such as units, theme and reminder time

None of this is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Permissions

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET) is part of the app framework. AirAlert does not send your data over the network.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS) are requested only if you turn on workout reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

AirAlert does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or storage permissions.

Backups

If you choose Export in Settings, AirAlert creates a backup file with your progress and jump log and hands it to the destination you pick, such as a file manager or cloud drive. The app does not upload it anywhere itself. Import reads only the file you select.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you use Reset program in Settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Android's automatic app backup is turned off for AirAlert, so your data is not copied to Google's servers by the system.

Children

AirAlert is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Changes that affect how data is handled will also be noted in the app's release notes.
