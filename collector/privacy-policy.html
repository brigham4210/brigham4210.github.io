Collector Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Collector is a personal tracker for license plates and coin designs. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

Data collected by the developerNone
Data shared with third partiesNone (see Network use)
Accounts or sign-inNone
Ads, analytics or crash reportingNone
Where your data livesOn your device only

What is stored

Your checklist progress and any photos you add are saved only on your device (in the app's local storage). They are never sent to the developer or anyone else. Backups you export are files you control.

Network use

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your device's IP address is visible to Google when those fonts are fetched, under Google's privacy policy. No other data leaves your device.

Permissions

If you add a photo, Android lets you pick or take one; the photo stays on your device.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it has stored.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Use the developer contact email shown on the Google Play listing.
